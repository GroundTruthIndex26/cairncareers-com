How a weak answer becomes a strong one

The coach never invents a number. It asks the one question that would produce it.

Step 1, the gate

"Did you tutor or teach anyone?"

Easy to answer yes to. No blank box, ever.

Step 2, what you did

"I tutored some people in my psych class."

Anything counts here, however thin.

Step 3, the number

"Tutored ___ classmates weekly, and ___ of them raised a grade. How many were there, and how many improved?"

The blank stays blank until you fill it. If you have no number, it falls back to an honest scope description, which still beats club member.

The honesty rule

No invented numbers, ever

The app will press you for a figure and it will show you where the figure belongs. It will never write one for you. If you truly do not have one, the record falls back to a concrete description of scope, which is still specific and still stronger than a list of memberships.

Method and sources

The published AI-exposure number is built only from O*NET task data, Eloundou et al., and METR. Pay, growth, networking, portfolio, and clean-up are context and never move it. A 0–100 exposure scale.
