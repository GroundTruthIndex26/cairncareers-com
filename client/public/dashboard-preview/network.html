Warm paths

The loose ties you already have are the ones that get people hired.

A causal experiment across more than 20 million LinkedIn users found an inverted U. Moderately weak ties, the acquaintances you already have and undervalue, drive more job mobility than either close friends or strangers. This page finds exactly those and tells you what to say.1

The safety line

Only your own uploaded contacts

Contacts arrive by export from you, never by a live connection to any platform. Nothing is scraped, no account is ever accessed on your behalf, and no claim is made about anyone's private network. Networking is context and never moves the AI-exposure number.

Method and sources

  1. Rajkumar et al. (2022), Science, a causal test of the strength of weak ties
  2. NACE, Job Outlook 2026

The published AI-exposure number is built only from O*NET task data, Eloundou et al., and METR. Pay, growth, networking, portfolio, and clean-up are context and never move it. A 0–100 exposure scale.
